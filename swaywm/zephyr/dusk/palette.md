Zephyr Dusk has no official Breeze equivalent — it's a hand-tuned dim
mid-tone that sits between Zephyr Light and Zephyr Dark (closer to Dark,
since "dusk" reads as early-night dimness rather than a neutral gray
midpoint), for working comfortably in low light without the full contrast
jump of Zephyr Dark. All neutrals are interpolated from the Light/Dark
pair; every accent hue is untouched so state colors (swaylock ring, mako
urgency, etc.) stay identical across all three variants.

Desktop wallpaper (`~/.img/wallpapers/zephyr_dusk_desktop.png`, soft overlapping
translucent circles in the 6 accent hues) and swaylock background
(`zephyr_dusk_lockscreen.png`, a subtle radial glow, surface1 → crust) are both
generated by `~/dotfiles/bin/palette_wallpaper.py --palette zephyr_dusk`
(desktop used `--pattern circles`; the lockscreen glow was hand-tuned with extra
noise dithering to avoid gradient banding, see git history for the exact script).
