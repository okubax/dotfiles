**No official Nord Light exists** — nordtheme.com ships Polar Night/Snow
Storm/Frost/Aurora as one dark-first system. This variant takes the
approach several community "Nord Light" ports use: invert which range
plays background vs. text (Snow Storm nord4–nord6 → backgrounds, Polar
Night nord0/nord3 → text), keep every Frost/Aurora accent exactly as-is.
`link` is the one accent that gets a variant-specific weight — `nord10`
(darker Frost blue) instead of `nord9`, for better contrast against the
light background, mirroring how Zephyr's `link` differs from its `accent`
for the same reason. `bg-panel-alt` is the one hand-interpolated value
(nord4→nord3 midpoint), needed because this port wants more background
layers than Nord's four canonical shades per range provide.

Desktop wallpaper (`~/.img/wallpapers/nord_light_desktop.png`) and swaylock
background (`nord_light_lockscreen.png`, a subtle radial glow, bg →
bg-panel) are both generated by `~/dotfiles/bin/palette_wallpaper.py
--palette nord_light` (desktop used `--pattern circles`; the lockscreen
glow was hand-tuned with extra noise dithering to avoid gradient banding).
