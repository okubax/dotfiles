Nord's own Polar Night range shifted up one step from `../dark/palette.md`
(bg=`nord1` instead of `nord0`, and so on) — a dimmer, lower-contrast dark
theme for comfortable low-light use, same "dusk = not full darkness"
principle as every other theme's Dusk here. There's no official Nord
equivalent to check this against (same caveat as Zephyr/Harmattan Dusk).

Since shifting up one step leaves only `nord3` covering three of this
port's five background roles, one value — the `nord3`→`nord4` midpoint,
`#929AA9` — is hand-interpolated and reused for `bg-panel-alt`,
`bg-header`, and `border`.

Desktop wallpaper (`~/.img/wallpapers/nord_dusk_desktop.png`) and swaylock
background (`nord_dusk_lockscreen.png`, a subtle radial glow, bg →
bg-panel) are both generated by `~/dotfiles/bin/palette_wallpaper.py
--palette nord_dusk` (desktop used `--pattern circles`, alpha boosted for
visibility; the lockscreen glow was hand-tuned with extra noise dithering
to avoid gradient banding).
