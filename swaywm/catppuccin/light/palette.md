Upstream Catppuccin's own, unmodified Latte flavor — the only official
light flavor, used here as the `light` slot. See `../dark/palette.md` for
why Catppuccin's accents (unlike every other theme here) are *not* held
constant across variants — Latte's `blue`/`red`/`green` etc. are all
genuinely different hues from Macchiato's and Mocha's upstream, chosen for
correct contrast against a light background rather than reused as-is.

Desktop wallpaper (`~/.img/wallpapers/catppuccin_light_desktop.png`) and
swaylock background (`catppuccin_light_lockscreen.png`, a subtle radial
glow, base → surface1) are both generated by
`~/dotfiles/bin/palette_wallpaper.py --palette latte` (desktop used
`--pattern circles`; the lockscreen glow was hand-tuned with extra noise
dithering to avoid gradient banding).
