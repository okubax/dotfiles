Upstream Catppuccin's own, unmodified Macchiato flavor — the second-darkest
of the four official flavors, used here as the `dusk` slot (a dimmer,
lower-contrast alternative to Mocha, same "dusk = comfortable low-light"
role every other theme in this repo gives it). See `../dark/palette.md` for
why Catppuccin's accents (unlike every other theme here) are *not* held
constant across variants — Macchiato's `blue` genuinely differs from
Mocha's and Latte's upstream, and that's preserved rather than flattened.

Desktop wallpaper (`~/.img/wallpapers/catppuccin_dusk_desktop.png`) and
swaylock background (`catppuccin_dusk_lockscreen.png`, a subtle radial
glow, base → surface1) are both generated by
`~/dotfiles/bin/palette_wallpaper.py --palette macchiato` (desktop used
`--pattern circles`, alpha boosted for visibility; the lockscreen glow was
hand-tuned with extra noise dithering to avoid gradient banding).
