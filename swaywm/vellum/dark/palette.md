# Vellum Dark palette

Strict grayscale (R=G=B in every value below) -- no hue anywhere in this
scheme, by design. a narrow, compressed range that never touches pure black or pure white -- the brightest tone available (accent) is still a soft off-white/off-black, not an extreme. A graphite-on-paper feel.

## Monochrome hue collapse

The app configs in this directory also reference `cyan` and `orange`
(kitty's ANSI slots, vim's `DiffChange`/`Structure` groups, waybar's
`@cyan` token). With no hue to give them a distinct identity, they fold
onto the nearest role that already exists rather than inventing new gray
steps no other role uses: `cyan -> link`, `orange -> yellow`.

## Semantic roles without color

`red`/`green`/`yellow`/`purple` don't mean literal red/green/yellow/purple
here -- they're the same *roles* (negative/positive/neutral/visited) the
other four families use, just resolved to distinct points on the gray
ramp instead of distinct hues. Where a role needs to grab attention
(focus, selection, urgent), `accent` sits at the ramp's boldest tone
available in this scheme (see above) rather than at a hue.

Wallpaper (`~/.img/wallpapers/vellum_dark_desktop.png`) and
swaylock background (`vellum_dark_lockscreen.png`) are generated by
`~/.cfg-tzevaot/bin/scheme_wallpaper.py`, a standalone generator built
for these three schemes' role-based palettes (see its own docstring) --
independent of `palette_wallpaper.py`, which the other five families use.
